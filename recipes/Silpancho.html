Silpancho recipe

Description

This traditional Bolivian dish from Cochabamba features a large, thinly pounded beef schnitzel, seasoned and coated in breadcrumbs, then fried until crispy. It is served over a generous bed of white rice and boiled, sliced potatoes, topped with a fried egg and a fresh, vibrant sarza (a relish of diced tomatoes, onions, and locoto peppers).

Ingredients

  • 4 thin beef steaks (pounded flat)
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 4 large potatoes, boiled and sliced
  • 2 cups cooked white rice
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tomatoes, finely diced
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced

Steps

  1. Prepare the meat: Season the beef steaks with salt and pepper. Place them over breadcrumbs and pound them with a meat mallet (or a heavy kitchen tool) until they are very thin and completely covered in crumbs.
  2. Fry potatoes and meat:In a skillet with hot oil, lightly fry the boiled potato slices until golden. Remove them, and in the same oil, fry the thin meat patties for about 2 minutes per side until crispy.
  3. Make the sarza:Mix the diced tomatoes and onions in a small bowl with a touch of oil and salt.
  4. Assemble:Serve a base of white rice and fried potatoes. Place the crispy meat on top, add a fried egg over the meat, and crown the dish with the fresh sarza.
