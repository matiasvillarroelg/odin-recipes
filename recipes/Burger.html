Burger recipe


Description

This classic homemade burger features a juicy, perfectly seared beef patty nestled inside a soft, toasted bun. Layered with melted cheddar cheese, crisp lettuce, ripe tomato slices, and a touch of savory sauce, it delivers a timeless and mouthwatering combination of textures and flavors

Ingredients

  • 1 burger bun (brioche or sesame), split and toasted
  • 1/3 lb (150g) ground beef patty
  • 1 slice of cheddar cheese
  • 1 lettuce leaf
  • 2 slices of ripe tomato
  • 3-4 slices of pickles
  • 1 tbsp ketchup and mayonnaise mixture

Steps

  1. Form and season:Shape the ground beef into a patty slightly wider than the bun and season both sides generously with salt and pepper
  2. Toast the bun:Lightly butter the cut sides of the bun and toast them in a skillet until golden brown, then set aside.
  3. Cook the patty:Cook the patty in a hot skillet for 3-4 minutes per side. Place the cheese slice on top during the last minute and cover with a lid to melt.
  4. Assemble:Spread the sauce on the bottom bun. Layer the lettuce, tomato, cheesy patty, and pickles, then top with the upper bun.
